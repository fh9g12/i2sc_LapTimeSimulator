Commands to type/paste into xfoil.exe (launched via xfoil_hang_demo.bat)
to reproduce the known-non-convergent case used for the teaching demo:
NACA2412, AoA=2deg, Re~1.53e6, Mach~0.131 (a 0.5m chord at 100mph).

Confirmed (independently of MATLAB/open.xfoil, directly against this
xfoil.exe) to genuinely oscillate rather than converge at this AoA/Re --
CL alternates between two fixed values every iteration, forever,
regardless of iteration cap. Nearby AoAs (0, 1, 3, 6, 8, 10) all
converge fine at this same Re, so it's a narrow, specific trouble spot,
not a generally broken case.

Type these one at a time (press Enter after each):

naca 2412
                <- (blank line)
                <- (blank line)
oper
re 1.53019e+06
mach 0.131366
visc
iter 150
pacc
                <- (blank line, answers "polar save filename" prompt)
                <- (blank line, answers "polar dump filename" prompt)
alfa 2

At this point it will iterate, print "VISCAL: Convergence failed", and
sit at the .OPERva prompt. To hold it open for the demo: just stop here
-- don't type "quit". The window (and process) will stay exactly as it
is until you close it yourself (close the window, Ctrl+C in the console,
or type "quit" when you're ready to end the demo).

If you want it to keep visibly "working" rather than sitting idle after
the failure, you can instead type "alfa 2" again (repeatedly) to re-run
the same failing point -- each attempt takes a couple of seconds at
iterCap=150 (measured ~2.2ms/iteration on this machine).
